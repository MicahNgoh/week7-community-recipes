# Mango Smoothie

**Category:** drinks  
**Prep time:** 5 minutes  
**Cook time:** 0 minutes  
**Servings:** 2  
**Difficulty:** Easy  

> Thick, sweet, and bright — this is what a good mango smoothie should taste like, with nothing unnecessary added.

---

## Ingredients

- 2 cups (330 g) frozen mango chunks (or fresh mango, frozen overnight)
- 1 cup (240 ml) full-fat coconut milk or whole milk
- ½ cup (120 ml) plain yogurt (Greek or regular)
- 1 tbsp honey or maple syrup (optional — taste first)
- ½ tsp fresh ginger, grated (optional)
- Juice of ½ lime

---

## Instructions

1. Add all ingredients to a blender in this order: liquid first (coconut milk), then yogurt, then frozen mango. This order helps the blender move without getting stuck.
2. Blend on high for 45–60 seconds until completely smooth. If the blender struggles, add 2 tbsp of liquid at a time and blend again.
3. Taste the smoothie. Add honey if the mango is not sweet enough, or more lime juice if it needs brightness.
4. Pour into two glasses and serve immediately.

---

## Notes

- **Frozen vs. fresh mango:** Frozen mango produces a thicker, colder smoothie without needing ice (which dilutes flavor). If using fresh mango, add ½ cup of ice.
- **Consistency:** For a thicker smoothie, use less liquid. For a drinkable consistency, add liquid in small amounts until you reach the right texture.
- **Dairy-free:** Substitute the yogurt with an equal amount of coconut milk or a dairy-free yogurt alternative.
- **Prep ahead:** Blend and store in a sealed jar in the fridge for up to 24 hours. Shake or stir before drinking.

---

## About This Recipe

I first had a version of this at a market in Accra and spent two years trying to recreate it. The lime juice is the detail that unlocks everything — it cuts the sweetness and makes the mango flavor more vivid.

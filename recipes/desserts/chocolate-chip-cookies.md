# Chocolate Chip Cookies

**Category:** desserts  
**Prep time:** 15 minutes + 30 minutes chilling  
**Cook time:** 12 minutes  
**Servings:** 24 cookies  
**Difficulty:** Easy  

> Crisp at the edges, chewy in the center, and loaded with chocolate — the benchmark all other cookies are measured against.

---

## Ingredients

- 2¼ cups (285 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp fine salt
- 1 cup (225 g) unsalted butter, at room temperature
- ¾ cup (150 g) granulated sugar
- ¾ cup (165 g) packed light brown sugar
- 2 large eggs, at room temperature
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 cups (340 g) semi-sweet chocolate chips or roughly chopped dark chocolate

---

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt. Set aside.
2. In a large bowl, beat the butter with both sugars using a hand mixer or stand mixer on medium speed for 3–4 minutes until light and fluffy. Scrape down the sides of the bowl halfway through.
3. Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition. Add the vanilla extract and beat for 30 more seconds.
4. Reduce the mixer to low and add the flour mixture in three additions, mixing only until just combined after each. Do not overmix.
5. Fold in the chocolate chips by hand with a spatula.
6. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes (up to 72 hours — the longer the rest, the deeper the flavor).
7. When ready to bake, preheat the oven to 190°C (375°F). Line two baking sheets with parchment paper.
8. Scoop the dough into balls about 1½ tablespoons each (roughly the size of a golf ball). Place them 5 cm (2 inches) apart on the prepared baking sheets.
9. Bake for 10–12 minutes until the edges are set and golden but the centers still look slightly underdone. They will firm up as they cool.
10. Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes before transferring to a wire rack.

---

## Notes

- **Room temperature butter matters.** Cold butter will not cream properly; melted butter produces a flatter, crisper cookie. Room temperature is the goal.
- **Do not skip the chill.** Chilling prevents excessive spreading and intensifies the flavor. 30 minutes is the minimum; overnight is better.
- **Storage:** Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days, or freeze unbaked dough balls for up to 3 months — bake straight from frozen, adding 2 minutes to the cook time.
- **Thicker cookies:** Roll the dough balls slightly taller than wide before baking.

## About This Recipe

This is the recipe that turned me into someone who always has cookie dough in the freezer. The secret is the brown sugar — it keeps the cookies chewy for days, long after they come out of the oven.

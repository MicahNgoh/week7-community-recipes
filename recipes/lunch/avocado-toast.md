# Avocado Toast

**Category:** lunch  
**Prep time:** 10 minutes  
**Cook time:** 5 minutes  
**Servings:** 2  
**Difficulty:** Easy  

> Simple to make and endlessly adaptable — a proper base recipe that you will personalize every time.

---

## Ingredients

**Base:**
- 2 thick slices of sourdough or whole-grain bread
- 2 ripe avocados
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- ¼ tsp flaky sea salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- Pinch of red chili flakes

**Optional toppings (pick one or combine):**
- 2 poached or fried eggs
- 4 cherry tomatoes, halved
- Thinly sliced radishes
- A drizzle of extra-virgin olive oil
- Fresh herbs (coriander, chives, or basil)

---

## Instructions

1. Toast the bread slices until golden and firm — they need to hold the topping without going soggy. A toaster works; a dry pan over medium-high heat works better.
2. While the bread toasts, halve and pit the avocados. Scoop the flesh into a bowl.
3. Add the lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Mash with a fork to your preferred texture — some people like it chunky, others prefer it smooth. Taste and adjust seasoning.
4. Spread the mashed avocado thickly onto the hot toast.
5. Add your chosen toppings.
6. Finish with a pinch of chili flakes and serve immediately.

---

## Notes

- **Ripe avocados:** A ripe avocado yields gently to thumb pressure without feeling mushy. If your avocados are hard, leave them on the counter for 1–2 days.
- **Lemon vs. lime:** Lime juice gives a slightly sharper result — both work.
- **Make it a meal:** Add a poached egg on top for extra protein. The runny yolk mixes into the avocado and acts as a sauce.
- **Goes soggy fast:** Assemble just before eating. The toast and avocado do not hold well together once combined.
